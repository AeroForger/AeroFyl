# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone working on AeroFyl - contributors, reviewers, and maintainers -
agrees to keep the project a place where technical work comes first. We
judge ideas by how well they serve the language pillars (see
`CONTRIBUTING.md`), not by who proposes them.

## Expected behavior

- Review code, not people. Criticize implementations with specifics:
  file paths, failing tests, measured numbers.
- Accept "no" when a change conflicts with the pillars. A polite technical
  objection is final until new evidence appears.
- Keep discussions on-topic and in the open: issues and pull requests, not
  side channels.
- Respect the `experimental/` boundary: it is personal exploration space.
  Do not open drive-by PRs against it or demand it follow project process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussion into personal disputes.
- Submitting plagiarized code or misrepresenting authorship.
- Reporting security issues publicly instead of privately (see `SECURITY.md`).

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainer
(AeroForger). Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from
a written warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project,
depending on severity and repetition. Maintainers may remove offending
content without prior notice to protect the community.
